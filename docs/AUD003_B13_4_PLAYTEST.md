## Audit and minimal corrections
- A pending heavy attack is represented in the fighter's narrow effect line (10px, nowrap, ellipsis). Several statuses can obscure it; multiple identical enemy labels are ambiguous; last-action feedback competes with current decision. Added a dedicated persistent warning in turn-info, groups pending attacks by threatened target/count, includes escape; threat target state moved first, duplicate NPCs identified by ordinal within side/id. Existing effects and selection remain.
- Existing choose(j) executes sole legal action even when player already selected incompatible target. This silently changes target: e.g. selected enemy + Liora teamcover (sole legal actor target). Fix: when incompatible prior selection, enter target-choice mode, highlight eligible target, require tap. A truly unselected singleton still fires on one tap. Both target-first and skill-first supported.
- Skill detail for signal/assist previously gave an arrow with no verb. Now displays “Ускорит:” or “Поможет:” and conditional preview; target selection instruction explicit. No promise of extra turn. Actual preview in fixture Sava requests 250, only 70 meter effective because cap; this illustrates why previous proposal of +400 was rejected.
- No general pop-up before each action, no mandatory tutorials, no changes to model. Avoid hiding actionable information solely in desktop title. Cooldowns remain visible; full trace and forecasts remain in diagnostic tabs. Source-level concern: duration and actual likely damage are still not sufficiently explicit, and grouped warning may need compact handling in extreme multi-target layouts. Need inspect screenshots before more UI changes, do not tune typography blind.

## Human protocol — no scripted hints during first pass
Open lab in landscape, first use existing preset “Первый бой · 2×2”, then “Защита подопечного”, then “Сорвать побег”. Play manually, no forecast, no auto, no help until first attempt. Observe whether player understands objective before first move; identifies who threatens whom; understands why skill is disabled; can explain guard vs interrupt vs kill; understands signal recipient and initiative, outcome/restart. Record taps, decision pauses, wrong-target attempts, outcome, what player expected vs actual. Do not read this answer table as a tutorial before first attempt. Second pass allow journal/forecast and compare explanation. Repeat on actual iPhone Safari. Candidate quality thresholds: no accidental action on wrong target, no clipping critical information, essential actions 1–2 taps, novice explains at least two viable responses without prompting after first example, can recover from failure and repeat. These are proposed acceptance criteria, not measured success.
