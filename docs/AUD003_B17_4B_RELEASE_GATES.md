- Boss: current test captain 80 resistance does not itself prevent Art disruption; combat-core applies disrupted-plan directly after hit (not resistance roll). Recommend explicit encounter-defined immunity only for phase cancellation; leave damage and other statuses according to existing rules, visible immune feedback, defense/taunt/Nada prediction remain useful. Do not make all bosses immune to everything or silently modify NPC stats. Must choose whether immune Art hit gets a bounded fallback weaken; recommend no additional fallback until tested, since damage still resolves. Test immune/nonimmune, misses, zero phase skip, UI, auto, waves.

Recommend candidate monotonic cover .65/.68/.71/.74/.77/.80 and passive cap .85/.88/.91/.94/.97/1.00 with 40% reduction and Protection conversion unchanged. These are explicitly **unvalidated** proposals: compare vs frozen B14.6 at equal real gear L1–6, pressure and uneven gears; cap may be nonbinding often, so reject or revise if upgrades are effectively dead. Alternative pause paid pilot upgrades for unchanged effects until design approved; no changes to production economy. Do not use higher mitigation rate or more reactions without testing.

Recommend playtest both from existing no-reward arena and retain story composition until acceptance, not treat higher damage as depth. FP/I unchanged.
