## 2. Пересмотр 15 действий
| Герой | II — самостоятельная ability | III — командная возможность (reserve) | V — высшая (тот же reserve) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Рен | Обход: одиночный hit обходит существующий заряд guard, не снимает/не расходует его, сохраняет DEF/Protection | Провести напарника: один обычный удар указанного союзника проходит так же | Сорвать прикрытие: снять конкретный guard или разрешённый taunt/screen link, без damage; открывает возможность всему отряду |
| Арт | Перехват: полная смена фактической цели одного прямого удара | Передать резерв: перенести часть имеющейся Protection между Артом и живым союзником, без создания ресурса | Последний рубеж: оплатить минимальный lethal HP loss, если достаточно здоровья |
| Нада | Контрплан: выбор конкретного enemy с двумя meaningful legal actions, зафиксировать его лучший допустимый альтернативный план | Предупредить напарника: защитить от одного объявленного secondary negative status, урон не отменяется | Разрыв командования: пресечь одно действие поддержки других противников/подтверждённое командное событие; сам enemy ходит |
| Лиора | Отвод: убрать выбранного соратника из доступных прямых целей до его следующего естественного действия; AoE сохраняется | Собрать отряд: снять один выбранный существующий вид снимаемого negative status с живых союзников | Вернуть в строй: recoverable KO only, не dead, не переписывать fail |
| Сава | Эстафета: вместо своего хода один раз за бой вызвать доступную special выбранного not-ready союзника | Переставить очередь: обмен готовности двух других живых not-ready союзников, если меняется реальный forecast | Синхронный приказ: выровнять время готовности двух союзников с сохранением суммы meter |
**Суть:** personal action II доступна отдельно от basic/special/powerful, никогда не требует trust для их обычных уровней. III и V — альтернативные инструменты, V не обязан быть универсально сильнее III. IV — две специализации командования в операциях, не новый боевой слот. Без suitable context способность может быть недоступна, базовый kit остаётся полноценным. Не считать пять NPC с guard доказательством, что Рен востребован в половине реальных боёв. Если реальная частота низкая, заменить способность, а не искусственно давать щит каждому врагу.
### Рен
Guard уже есть в native damage pipeline; 5 из 10 фактических NPC archetypes имеют guard action (pass guard, signaller, captain, sarkh convoy guard, laun ford guard). 2 heal archetypes. Native actorTargetIndices применяет taunt только при enemy→ally, поэтому заявленный в 0.1 запрет ally→enemy не существует. Не вводить его глобально. Для II только реально активный guard; не bypass DEF/Protection, не ignore hit/crit. Обычный удар расходует guard и открывает другим цель, обход сохраняет guard для дальнейших ударов — осмысленная цена. В III Рен платит действием за одно право напарника до его следующего action, не бесплатную атаку. V снимает конкретное защитное состояние вместо нанесения удара, mode policy для hard scripted protection. Native assist/followups не получают право автоматически. Screen link, если когда-нибудь потребуется в авторском encounter, опциональный extension, не блокер C4/R1. 
### Арт
II redirect prior to target defense/hit/status resolution, one root, no AoE, no scripted harm, no double interception (legacy insurance/Combat2 reaction) on redirected root. III transfer: selected donor Art or ally, recipient other, explicit amount=min(requested, donor.currentProtection, receiver.maxProtection-current). Proposed cap for first pilot 65% Art effective attack (tuning only); source from real reserve, no healing, not same as base restore. Can move from protected Art to threatened ally or vice versa; never use on KO, self, no deficit. V only if complete rescue affordable: if Art HP insufficient, normal ally KO, no pointless sacrifice killing both; reserve was already committed and spent. If exact cost equals Art HP, Art KO and ordinary objective fail still occurs. Compare Art 20/ally30/incoming55 → no transfer, ally KO; Art40 → transfer26, Art14/ally1. Not two-pass mitigation. 
### Нада
Need generalize current EnemyDecision into pure rankedCandidates (with current legality, target/support usefulness), shared with forecast/resolve. It currently calls actorAbilityReady which requires meter>=1000; forecast must use a side-effect-free next-turn readiness view, no fake actual meter mutation. Native prepared logic currently only trail interceptor and ridge slinger; preserve these and normal AI. Show next intended ability/target as conditional forecast to all ranks, not omniscient promise. II chooses alternative ability (not arbitrary target, not deliberate heal full HP or weak illegal action), highest-ranked other meaningful legal candidate; lock only selected enemy's next intent at action commit, if invalid by resolution due cooldown/target/KO/phase, replan once under same legality; no skipped turn and no refund. No second forced choice before resolution. III chooses ally + one explicitly visible debuff family and one enemy root: prevent that secondary effect once, damage unchanged, not shield/taunt/redirect; cannot protect multiple allies, cannot undo existing status; only if ordinary attack could apply it. V reserve disables one already declared cross-enemy support event (heal/guard other foe, group support or authored reinforcement), chooses valid normal fallback at resolution; no universal silence, no boss phase cancellation; no artificial extra event created solely for Nada. Existing read-move/interrupt remains valuable; test against regular debuff attackers, guard/healer group, basic-only threat and phase-locked boss. 
### Лиора
II target leaves single-target selection only until its next natural ready event, returns **before** normal action (no skip and no extra action). AoE/status/global damage still apply; no automatic healing, no assist/helper while sheltered; no last active ally/mandatory protect anchor/stacking shelters; if taunt active, sheltered ally no longer taunt target, enemy selects another legal target; mode explicit, on wave change return no meter changes. This is threat distribution, not Art redirect or invulnerability. III one chosen currently present removable debuff family (weaken, slowed, disrupted-plan; read-move only if registry allows); one Liora action, one living support/guardian partner, no partner action granted; remove chosen status once per eligible ally, no healing, not immunity or all-status purge. Require at least two actual affected allies, otherwise use normal action. Handoff-objective moved to IV. V **not enabled until life-state contract**: actual core currently emits death at HP0 and immediately checks protected-fallen/terminal; recoverable vs true death must be declared by mode, no resurrection of true death, no scripted/anchor failure rewind, no revive after terminal, prior KO recorded for score/proof even if recovered. Candidate restore20% HP, Protection/meter0, old cooldowns retained; explicit owner confirmation before adoption. No hidden magic fact.
### Сава
II relay works with one other ally. One activation/battle (separate personal budget), only ready-cooldown **special** of another not-ready living ally, selected legal target. Sava spends her own natural turn, no own attack, partner cooldown consumed, no partner meter or natural own-turn ticks, no recursive assist/trust/extra-action ability. Partner still gets later natural turn with spent cooldown. Not arbitrary powerful or basic; avoid permanent strong-hero basic spam; avoid exploiting low-level Sava by compare lineup with/without Sava across mixed progression. III swap two not-ready allies excluding Sava, both meters conserved, actual order forecast must change, same native effective speed/tie-break. V select two others not-ready. Set common readiness time t=(2000-m1-m2)/(s1+s2), new meters 1000-s1*t and 1000-s2*t, reject if either negative/ready or no real timing change. Sum conserved; fast units can make combination invalid. All natural turns, no reserved token/wait loop, enemy can go first (including a tie under existing index rule); future slow/boost/death can disrupt synchronization, preview explicitly conditional. This is NOT guaranteed consecutive turns under arbitrary effects; do not claim one if future state changes. Sample fixed speeds use actual InitiativeModel.nextActor/finishTurn, verify enemy earlier still acts and allies each consume natural meter. Compare full battle action counts, not just meter conservation (phase changes can alter count within finite horizon).
## 3. Shared reserve and UX
Choose ONE III or V plan for squad before battle; no separate ultimate budget. One legal action consumes it for whole battle/all waves, invalid/cancel no consumption, no new turn. II Sava relay has separate per-hero budget and cannot be copied by any action. V should change options, not invalidate III: Art transfer before predictable damage vs emergency save, Nada warning vs enemy support disruption, Sava reorder vs simultaneous timing, Liora cleanse vs recoverable KO. If telemetry shows V always selected for generic power, redesign, not add another slot. 4 visible fixed personal buttons + at most 1 reserve chip on eligible actor, no six per hero or contextual popup on every turn. Keep active hero/initiative/target feedback; compact rail for 844×320 and 844×390; 44px touch targets. Before battle sheet shows only eligible plans and reason unavailable, remembers choice. No special art generated now; actual render and device acceptance after native pilot. Long tap details, tap clear selection; no page scroll.

## 5. Enemy and objective architecture / operational specialization
Actual native catalog 10 archetypes; Combat2 prepared threats only two; screen not natively enforced on allies. Need generic **encounter affordances** using existing roles, not one custom enemy for every new trust ability. R1 pack: (A) normal guard+medic or signaller, native guard charges (Ren); (B) ordinary target threat + AoE option, escort or low-HP ally (Art/Liora); (C) actual prepared enemy plus normal debuff/support decisions (Nada), baseline telegraph shared; (D) mixed speeds and high threat with optional priority objective (Sava); (E) ordinary no special affordance baseline; (F) waves/phase-locked boss for boundaries, not artificial boss phase until proper design. Use existing NPC models/abilities, no lore invention. Compare each action against basic/special/powerful and same encounter without unlocked skill; prove at least two independent meaningful use cases for each II, one situation where standard action wins, no hard requirement to own trust hero, and no farming proof via no-op. If mechanics unused in normal human runs, redesign before release, do not tweak enemy stats just to force use. V/III can be contextual but should be meaningful across several modes.
IV **not 10 new screens or passive timers**. Reuse operation grammar: actor responsibility, preparation→approach→battle(s)→result; templates add explicit outcome facts and objective. Preserve baseline path. One initial production pilot in C5: Art protection/withdrawal vs ordinary assault in existing OPS-002-like task, but don't simply reuse C2 faster hold. Existing SignalOperationCore already has survey/extraction, quiet/cordon, final cover/ledger/leave/both; do not advertise existing options as trust. New first-clear alternative: instead of requiring all threats defeated, accept a specific protection/withdrawal outcome with a maintained protected actor and genuine danger window; outcome records preserved group vs secured intelligence/suppressed signal, no both unless earned by separate authored conditions. Baseline can also achieve each central goal via different tactics; no mandatory trust. Use meaningful first-time branch narrative/access, shared reward receipt, no replay award, no generic shortcuts on recurring resource clues. If alt clear is 4 vs 12 actions for same reward and recurring farm, reject; require actual reward/minute and failure-risk comparison under manual/auto/mastered simulation, no padding with meaningless enemies/time tax. Concrete route/story/receipt IDs only after actual C5 adapters. Remainder Ren route, Nada verified information, Liora rescue/handoff, Sava dependent stages implemented as content families, not promised now. One primary specialization selected when applicable, no new queue, no hours lock; optional route effects not currency multiplier.
