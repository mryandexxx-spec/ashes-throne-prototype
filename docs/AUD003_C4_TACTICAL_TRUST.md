## Mechanics
Art II: standard unchanged (one 50% incoming-hit guard, restores protection up to 65% physical attack); focused: two guard charges on selected other living ally, zero restore; shared: one charge selected ally + one Art, zero restore. Max existing charge, no additive stacking. Same special slot/cooldown/turn. Shared explicitly names Art, not arbitrary hidden target. Same two charge budget focused/shared; choice depends on concentration vs divided incoming threats; standard can be better against a depleted protection pool. Exact numbers experimental, no stat bonus or extra guard duration. Avoid automatic extra passive re-trigger.
III: one chosen squad plan (Art initiator + Ren or Nada from explicit compatible ID list, lab Ren only); substitute ordinary Art basic with 55% of effective basic multiplier and native depth-1 assist (65% partner attack capped by Art attack). One successful legal command per entire battle, not wave; no extra action or meter, partner must live, target valid, no recursion. Native assist already reports damage/death; primary lethal can waste assist; no free follow-up after target dies. Normal basic remains. Misses consume legal action/plan. Preview and execution use same resolver. Rank checks in lab configure, immutable battle snapshot; production authorization belongs to future C5 adapter not client options. No automatic plan or AI support in this pilot.
