## Art critique before intake
The source-listed models are not an art pass. Mountainside 288K and Boulder 124K are too heavy without source LOD selection; Rock Face 02 30K promising but geology consistency must be seen. Fort kit is colonial fort, not a city: extract individual compatible retaining wall/arch modules, reject battlements and iconic fortress look. Castle door risks medieval-European stereotype, reserve only; need convincing residential facade/awning, which this set does NOT solve. Fern alpha overdraw and climate; barrels/bench may look like stage dressing. No random scattering. Source preview is not actual import. Eight candidate sources chosen for coherent test, not final kit; all require actual rendered visual review, no new geographic facts.
## Pipeline
1. Lore/art brief -> source search -> license/creator and scale/camera role -> shortlist; record rejected alternatives. 2. Finite API intake verifies size and MD5 for every dependency, adds SHA256, retains original source+provider metadata. 3. Blender imports, reports mesh/material/image counts and real bounds/triangles, exports editable blend and GLB; renders two actual angles per candidate. Source and converted packages are isolated CI artifacts, no commit of raw binaries. 4. Human and strict artist review; choose compatible parts and separate LODs, judge imported materials and silhouette at gameplay scale. Never accept solely on green CI. 5. Only after kit gate, assemble one small authored scene in Blender with ART/NAV/MARKERS, source-based shapes; no procedural main art. Reuse #596 Babylon/Recast and Ren, separate NAV proxy offline bake and route verification if necessary; no solver edits. 6. Capture fixed camera at 844x390, 932x430, 844x320 in Chromium/WebKit, compare against Void Hunters (official environment devlog https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xkfAoXmsEx0); compare near/mid/far mass, visual navigation, silhouette, lived-in structural logic, controlled color and light, avoid darkness and IP copying. 7. After artistic gate 03C optimize based on measured transfer, decoded textures/VRAM, draw calls, frame time, lifecycle, no arbitrary global triangle budget. Preserve source/derivative hashes and modifications; independent source per model and stable IDs; no asset substitution by silent fallback.
